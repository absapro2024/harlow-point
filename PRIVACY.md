# Harlow Point: Shift Work — Privacy Policy
Effective: October 3, 2026

Harlow Point: Shift Work does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

- Your character name, choices, and save files are stored only on your device (local app storage). They never leave your device unless you copy an export code yourself.
- The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
- The app does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or health data.
- Deleting the app deletes all of its data.
